How to delete your account and your data

You can request the complete deletion of your account and all your data at any time, in two ways.

  • In the app: open Attos, tap Profile, then the gear at the top of the screen, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account and data. Confirm with Delete permanently. The app erases your workouts, history, measurements and the cloud backup, and disconnects your Google account.
  • By email: if you already uninstalled the app, write to contato@attosfit.com with the subject “Data deletion — Attos” and tell us the email of the Google account you used in the app. We handle it within 5 business days and confirm by email.

Deletion is permanent: the data cannot be recovered afterwards. Deleting the account does not cancel an active Attos PRO subscription; cancellation is done in Google Play.

If you use the app without an account, your data is stored on the device only. In that case, “Delete account and data” erases everything from the device, and uninstalling the app has the same effect.
